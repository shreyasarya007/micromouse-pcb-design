# Third-party sources and publication scope

This repository contains combined PCB/schematic designs, not a mirror of a CAD library collection. Attribution fields in the input designs are preserved. No third-party copyright or license notice was removed to conceal an origin.

Attribution is retained to **SnapMagic Search (formerly SnapEDA)** and to the original CAD creators where identified by the linked source records. SnapMagic's published policy identifies individual CAD downloads as [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) with **Design Exception 1.0**, permitting combined designs to be conveyed under the designer's chosen terms; public CAD sharing also calls for source attribution. See the [official FAQ and exception](https://www.snapeda.com/about/FAQ/) and [official licensing explanation](https://support.snapmagic.com/en/articles/2957814-what-is-the-license-for-symbols-and-footprints).

This preparation did not modify embedded symbol or footprint geometry. The supplied designs may already contain adaptations. Individual CAD author/version records were not available for every embedded component, so the source links and this limitation are retained rather than inventing authorship. Standalone downloaded CAD libraries and models are not included.

## KiCad standard libraries

The designs use standard-library namespaces including Switch and Connector_PinHeader_2.54mm. Credit to the **KiCad library contributors**. KiCad libraries use CC BY-SA 4.0 with a design exception; its [official license page](https://www.kicad.org/libraries/license/) distinguishes combined designs from redistributing a library collection. No KiCad standalone library collection is included here.

## Custom blocks and remaining provenance limits

Other user-supplied custom blocks are present in the native design files. Their individual authorship and source history were not independently documented in the provided material. This notice does not assert that those blocks are third-party, that they are infringement-free, or that every asset has been legally cleared. The owner should retain evidence of authorship/permission and review any institutional obligations before licensing or further redistribution.
